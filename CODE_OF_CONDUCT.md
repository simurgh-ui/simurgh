# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Simurgh UI contributors must help maintain a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive
community. Be considerate, welcome questions, give actionable feedback, respect differing
experiences, and focus disagreement on the work rather than the person.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, publication of
private information, and sustained disruption are unacceptable in project spaces or when
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers or GitHub's reporting tools.
Maintainers will protect reporter privacy where practical, investigate fairly, and may correct,
warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently remove participants according to the severity and
pattern of behavior. Maintainers must recuse themselves when they are involved in a report.

This policy is based on the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
